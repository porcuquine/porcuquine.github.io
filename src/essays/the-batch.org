#+TITLE: The Batch
#+SUBTITLE: After [[https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/44977/the-mill][The Mill]] by Edwin Arlington Robinson.

“There are no founders anymore.”\\
He turned the crowded screen her way;\\
The ventures came in by the score,\\
Each funded, launched, and live that day.\\
The money chose its own address,\\
Then named the firm and built the site;\\
The deck, the product, and the press\\
Were ready by the fall of night.\\

By noon his name had left the Book;\\
His public feed was clean and bare.\\
The launch page kept its polished look;\\
He’d signed away his final share.\\
No gap appeared where he had been;\\
The engine neither slowed nor failed.\\
The numbers held their course onscreen;\\
The round went on; the venture scaled.\\

His profile gone, she closed her own;\\
She named the thing she wished were made.\\
By dawn the venture stood alone;\\
No founder helped it ply its trade.\\
Across the world, unnumbered lights\\
Came on wherever wants were named;\\
The cities glittered through the nights,\\
Too bright to tell what had been gained.
