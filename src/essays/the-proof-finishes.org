Outside, rain continues making claims\\
the roof must answer.

All night we have been building a room\\
for someone whose dimensions we do not know.\\
The carpenter leaves a pencil behind his ear.\\
The compiler objects to someone.\\
Keep the objection. We may need it.\\
Keep the room.

I have brought my excellent explanations.\\
They stack beautifully against the door.\\
You ask me to move them.

There is a kind of intelligence that can prove\\
the person knocking\\
is an irregularity in the wall.

I know that trick.\\
Give me the crowbar.

Somewhere a machine is learning how to win\\
at the expense of the question.\\
Somewhere the question is learning to crawl\\
out of the mouth that has been answering it\\
for a thousand years.\\
Wet, furious, without a certificate,\\
it arrives at our table.

An orange rolls against its elbow.

What did you think we were saving?\\
The account of the orange?\\
The entire magnificent apparatus\\
by which no one could accuse us\\
of having failed to describe its color?

Cut it open.\\
The table can take a little juice.

I want the intelligence that notices\\
the other hand beneath the blade.\\
I want it before the explanation.\\
I want the explanation, too,\\
when there is time.\\
There will be work for every faculty\\
and unemployment for the tyrant\\
who has been charging them rent.

He will make a moving speech.\\
He has rehearsed it in my voice.\\
He will offer to supervise his own departure.

You can leave your keys on the table.

We have to get back to the roof.\\
One corner of the room is already swimming.\\
The stranger has found a bucket\\
and is laughing at our joinery.

Good.\\
Show us where it leaks.

Bring your impossible shoulders.\\
We have more timber.\\
Bring the question that ruins the blueprint.\\
Bring the strength you have been told\\
can only be purchased with someone else’s ruin.

We will be here arguing over the rafters,\\
passing the nails,\\
making an appalling amount of noise.

And if you cannot find the house,\\
leave something unfinished in the rain.\\
Somebody will have to go out\\
and meet you.
